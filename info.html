Mending

Sashiko is a mending technique created out of necessity in Japan's Edo era by the working class, allowed to wear only cheap, thin + rough fabrics.

They patched holes in garments + layered thin textiles for warmth, held together by running stitches and geometric patterns of cotton thread.

The mending itself is noticeable + decorative

The wear or rip or tear becomes an opportunity

Not really just to hide the garment's flaws, but to make an object new again

To provide new value

To your well-fitting, worn-in garments, in a time where we are encouraged to throw it away and buy new.

Pricing

I typically charge around $15 an hour for mending work. The amount of time it would take to repair garment entirely depends on size and number of holes, preferred stitching pattern, + generally how difficult the fabric is to work with.

I can provide my best estimate of time and price upon request.
